StarDew Project

Ready for some fun? Try guiding your bunny or chicken to face the yummy treats as they move! Or get creative, can you make interesting patterns in the fields by using the move controls to skip some plants?

Use these keyboard controls to help your animal face what they eat:

Bunny orientation: F (forward), H (left), G (right), B (backward)

Chicken orientation: T (forward), U (left), Y (right), N (backward)

Overview of all the other keyboard controls:

Camera Views: 1-Front, 2-Overhead, 3-Bunny Field, 4-Hen Field, 5-Back, 6-Ground level

Bunny: Move (S-Up, A-Left, W-Down, D-Right)| Chicken: Move (K-Up, J-Left, I-Down, L-Right)
